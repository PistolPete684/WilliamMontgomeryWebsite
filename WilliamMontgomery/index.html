Stand Up Commedy

By William Montgomery

William Montgomery

William Flournoy Montgomery is a stand‑up comedian known for his eccentric style, unpredictable delivery, and unforgettable presence on stage. Born in Memphis, Tennessee in 1978, Montgomery has carved out a unique niche in the comedy world by blending absurdist humor with sharp wit and a fearless approach to storytelling.

He first gained widespread attention through his recurring appearances on Kill Tony, the live comedy podcast hosted in Austin, Texas, where his offbeat characters and surreal one‑liners quickly made him a fan favorite. Standing at 6’1”, Montgomery’s towering frame and fiery red hair add to his distinctive stage persona, but it’s his ability to surprise audiences with unexpected twists that truly sets him apart.

Since beginning his career in the early 2010s, Montgomery has performed across the United States, building a reputation as one of the most original voices in modern stand‑up. His comedy often plays with the boundaries of reality, leaving audiences unsure of what’s scripted and what’s improvised, a hallmark of his style.

Today, Montgomery continues to tour, record podcasts, and connect with fans through his online platforms, including The William Montgomery Show. With his trademark catchphrase “I ain’t EVER GONNA STOP,” he embodies a relentless commitment to comedy that keeps audiences laughing and guessing.
